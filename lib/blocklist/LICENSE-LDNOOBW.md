# LDNOOBW word lists

`ldnoobw.ts` contains the German and English lists of the
[List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words](https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words)
by Shutterstock, Inc. and contributors, used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.

The lists were pruned for use as a display-name filter; the removed entries are recorded in the
same file. Everything else in this repository is under the MIT license, see `LICENSE` at the root.
